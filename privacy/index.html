Privacy policy.

How BRAVAS collects and uses your information.

BRAND MOOD IMAGERY

Last updated: November 10, 2026

BRAVAS ("we," "us") operates bravasvip.com, its application, reservation, artist and request forms (hosted by Tally), and the BRAVAS member portal at bravasvip.floot.app. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored securely, never in plain text)
  • Membership applications: name, mobile number, date of birth, contact preference, how you heard about us, and marketing preferences
  • Reservations: the night, arrival time, section and party size you request
  • Artist submissions: contact details, date of birth, act details, links, photos, press kits, and representative contacts you provide
  • Requests: song requests, private event inquiries, ideas and suggestions, with any contact details you include

How we use it

  • To review membership applications and contact you about them
  • To hold and check in your reservations at the door
  • To review artist submissions and promote booked performers (name, photo and act type appear on public event pages)
  • To respond to requests and private event inquiries
  • To send updates and promotions by email or text, only if you opted in

Text messages and email

If you opt in to texts, message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email. Opting out of marketing does not affect your membership or reservations.

Payments

Payments are processed on a secure, encrypted third-party checkout page. We do not see or store card numbers.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run BRAVAS, such as our hosting, email and payment providers.

Your choices

To update or delete your information, email info@bravasvip.com.
