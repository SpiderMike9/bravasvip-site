Frequently asked questions

Who can join?

Women 25 and older and men 30 and older. Every applicant completes an application and is reviewed by an owner.

Is every applicant accepted?

No. Membership is approval-based. An owner reviews each application personally.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are priority. We fill up fast, and door entry is only while we're under capacity, so we recommend booking ahead. Reservations are released 15 minutes after your arrival time. Reserve today.

How do I reserve?

Tap Reserve, pick your night, arrival time and party size, and the BRAVAS team confirms by text or email. Members get in free on regular nights. Guest cover varies by event and is paid at the door.

Do members get in free to every event?

Members get in free on all regular nights, including Members Monday, Spoken Word Wednesday, Throwback Thursday, Feel Good Friday, V.I.P Saturday and Sunday Funday. Some special events feature paid headliners or touring acts. Those shows are ticketed, and the price is listed on the event. Members always get priority access.

What do members get?

Free entry on regular nights, first access to ticketed shows, seats held all night, one free meal a month, priority reservations, automatic event and reservation notifications, and member-only events.

How much is membership?

$40 a month, renewing monthly, paid on Square's secure checkout. Pay here. Apply too, because membership becomes active once an owner approves you.

Can I come if I'm not a member?

Yes. Guests pay a cover charge at the door, and the amount varies by event. Door entry depends on capacity, so reserve online to hold your spot. You can visit as a guest while your application is pending.

What are your hours?

Monday: 3 PM – 12 AM (Members Monday)
Tuesday: Closed
Wednesday – Friday: 3 PM – 12 AM
Saturday: 3 PM – 1 AM (V.I.P Saturday)
Sunday: 3 PM – 12 AM (Sunday Funday)

I'm an artist — how do I get booked?

Submit on the Artists page with your samples. If it's a fit, we'll contact you or your representative to confirm a date and slot.

How do I contact BRAVAS?

Email info@bravasvip.com Staff login, or send us a request.
