Can I visit before becoming a member?

Yes. Guests may reserve a visit and pay the event’s cover at the door. Admission depends on capacity.

How does membership work?

Apply online. An owner reviews your application. Once approved, pay $40 per month to activate membership. Women 25+, men 30+.

Should I reserve ahead?

Yes. With 40 seats, advance reservations receive priority. Your party’s reservation is subject to confirmation. Arrive on time; reservations are released 15 minutes after the selected arrival time.
